FuelMate - Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: November 12, 2026
Package Name: com.fuelmate.fuelcalc
Developer: Saikase

1. Privacy Policy

Zero Personal Data Collection: FuelMate does NOT collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. We do not require account registration, and we do not access your contacts, precise GPS location, photos, or storage files.

Device Storage: Vehicle preferences and saved trips are saved locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage. You can clear this data at any time inside the app.

Third-Party Advertisements (Google AdMob): We use Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. Google AdMob may use anonymized mobile identifiers to serve relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your Android Settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

2. Terms of Service

FuelMate is designed as an estimation calculator for trip fuel costs based on published manufacturer economy metrics and public fuel station benchmarks. Actual fuel consumption may differ based on driving habits, vehicle load, and traffic conditions.
